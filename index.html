My Channel YT

A personal tool that publishes short animated videos to its owner's own YouTube channel.

It runs only on the owner's computer. Once or twice a week it makes one original short video, uploads it to the owner's channel as a private video, and sets its cover image. The owner reviews each video and makes it public by hand.

It is not a public service. Nobody else signs in to it, and it never touches anyone else's account.

Google permissions it asks for

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): upload a video to the owner's channel and set its thumbnail.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): check whether an upload has finished processing.
